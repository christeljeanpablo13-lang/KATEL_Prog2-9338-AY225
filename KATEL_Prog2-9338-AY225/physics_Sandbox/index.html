Crayon Physics

Physics Sandbox Puzzle Game

✏️ Draw shapes • Drop objects • Solve puzzles

📖 How to Play

✏️ Drawing

✏️Draw tool: Click and drag to draw crayon lines. Objects collide with them!
🎨Colors: Pick from the palette in the top bar.
📏Thickness: Use the slider to change line width.

🛠️ Tools

✋Drag: Click any physics object to move it around.
🗑️Erase: Click lines or objects to remove them.
❄️Freeze: Lock objects in place so they don't move.

🧩 Spawn Objects

⚽Ball: Bouncy circle.
📦Box: Heavy square block.
🐕Dog: Roaming companion.
🟢Goo: Squishy spring-connected blobs.
🪢Rope/Chain: Click and drag to create.
💣Bomb: Explodes after 2 seconds!
⭐Star: Collectible — needed in some levels!

🎮 Modes

🧩Level Mode: Solve physics puzzles with goals. Fewer draws = more stars!
🎨Free Mode: No rules — experiment and create!

🔊 Sound

🔔Click the 🔊 button (top right in-game) to toggle sound on/off.
